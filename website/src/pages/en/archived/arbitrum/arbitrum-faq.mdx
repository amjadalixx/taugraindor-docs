> [!IMPORTANT] TAU now runs on Ethereum Layer 2 (Arbitrum). Legacy L2 transfer tools have been deprecated in favor of a native L2-first experience.

Click [here](#billing-on-arbitrum-faqs) if you’d like to skip to the **Billing on Arbitrum** FAQs.

## Why does TAU use an L2?

By operating TAU on an L2, participants benefit from:

- Up to ~26x lower gas costs (typical vs. L1)
- Faster transaction finality
- Security inherited from Ethereum

Putting protocol contracts on L2 lets participants interact more frequently at a far lower cost. For example, Indexers (or analogous TAU node operators) can open/close workloads more often, developers can deploy and update workloads more easily, and token holders can perform on‑chain actions that were previously cost‑prohibitive on L1.

The community selected **Arbitrum One** as the production L2 for TAU after evaluating fees, ecosystem tooling, and security properties.

## What do I need to use TAU on L2?

TAU’s on‑chain interactions occur on **Arbitrum One**. You’ll need:

- **ETH on Arbitrum** to pay gas.
- Your **TAU assets** (or credits) available on Arbitrum to power paid actions.

If you already hold assets on Ethereum mainnet, you can bridge them to Arbitrum using one of the common bridges below:

- [Arbitrum Bridge](https://bridge.arbitrum.io/?l2ChainId=42161)
- [TransferTo](https://transferto.xyz/swap)

If you have other tokens on Arbitrum, you can swap them for what you need via a DEX such as Uniswap.

Once your balance is on Arbitrum, you can fund your TAU billing balance from the TAU Console.

To take advantage of L2 while using the docs/app, use the chain switcher to toggle to **Arbitrum**.

![Dropdown switcher to toggle Arbitrum](/img/arbitrum-screenshot-toggle.png)

## As a developer, data consumer, node operator, curator, or delegator, what should I do now?

All network activity for TAU is on **Arbitrum One**. Move your relevant balances and update your workflow to use Arbitrum endpoints. (If you’re migrating from L1, follow your internal migration guide.)

All network rewards and settlements now occur on Arbitrum.

## Were there risks associated with moving to L2?

All protocol contracts were thoroughly audited prior to deployment and the migration path was tested end‑to‑end in staging. A contingency plan exists to ensure a safe transition in the event of L2 incidents.

## Are existing L1 deployments still working?

Active protocol components now live on Arbitrum. If you still have legacy artifacts on L1, migrate them to Arbitrum to ensure full functionality going forward.

## Does TAU have contracts on Arbitrum?

Yes. TAU maintains deployed contracts on Arbitrum One for production use. (Your mainnet contracts remain operational where applicable, but new activity is on L2.)

## Billing on Arbitrum FAQs

## What do I need to do about my existing billing balance?

If you previously used L1 billing, your TAU billing balance has been migrated to Arbitrum (or can be bridged using the TAU Console). Your balance on Arbitrum will be used to pay for requests.

## How do I know my funds are safely on Arbitrum?

You can verify balances and transactions directly on Arbiscan by checking the relevant TAU billing/payment contract address.

## How do I know the bridge is secure?

Arbitrum’s canonical bridge and commonly used third‑party bridges have undergone extensive audits and public review. Always verify contract addresses and use well‑known interfaces.

## What if I’m adding fresh funds from my Ethereum mainnet wallet?

Use your preferred bridge to move assets to Arbitrum, then top up your TAU billing balance from the TAU Console. The flow supports bridging and funding your API keys in a single streamlined step.

Visit the **Billing** section for detailed instructions on adding, withdrawing, or acquiring funds on Arbitrum.
